# 7-Day Client Follow-Up Kit
## A practical copy-and-paste message pack for freelancers and small service businesses

**Version:** 1.0  
**Format:** Digital guide + editable message templates  
**Intended buyer:** Solo freelancers, repair shops, tutors, designers, consultants, and other small service providers who receive enquiries through WhatsApp, email, or social media.  
**Purpose:** Help a business respond consistently to enquiries without sounding pushy.  
**Important:** These are communication templates, not a promise of more sales. Adapt them to the customer's language, local rules, and the actual service offered.

---

## 1. Quick-start instructions

1. Replace every item in [square brackets] with accurate information.
2. Use the customer's name only if they provided it.
3. Send a reply promptly when practical; do not send repeated messages if the person has asked not to be contacted.
4. If a customer says “no”, “stop”, or otherwise declines, stop the follow-up sequence.
5. Never invent scarcity, discounts, testimonials, deadlines, or results.
6. Keep customer personal information private. Do not paste sensitive payment or identity details into public tools.
7. Track only the minimum information needed: date, enquiry topic, last reply, and next action.

## 2. First-response templates

### A. New enquiry — friendly and clear
**English**
> Hi [Name], thanks for contacting [Business]. I can help with [service/product]. To give you an accurate answer, could you tell me [one relevant question]? I’ll reply with the next steps once I have that.

**O‘zbekcha**
> Salom [Ism], [Biznes]ga murojaat qilganingiz uchun rahmat. [Xizmat/mahsulot] bo‘yicha yordam bera olaman. Sizga aniq javob berishim uchun [bitta kerakli savol]ni ayta olasizmi? Ma’lumotni olganimdan keyin keyingi qadamlarni yozaman.

### B. Enquiry received outside working hours
**English**
> Thanks for your message. We’ve received your enquiry. Our usual reply hours are [days and hours]. We’ll get back to you during that time. If your request is urgent, please [accurate alternative contact/process].

**O‘zbekcha**
> Xabaringizni oldik, rahmat. Odatdagi javob berish vaqtimiz: [kunlar va soatlar]. Shu vaqt ichida siz bilan bog‘lanamiz. Masala shoshilinch bo‘lsa, [haqiqiy muqobil aloqa/tartib]dan foydalaning.

### C. Need details before quoting
**English**
> I can prepare a clear estimate. Please share [detail 1] and [detail 2]. The final price may depend on [genuine factor]. I’ll explain any additional cost before you decide.

**O‘zbekcha**
> Sizga tushunarli narx hisobini tayyorlayman. Iltimos, [1-ma’lumot] va [2-ma’lumot]ni yuboring. Yakuniy narx [haqiqiy omil]ga bog‘liq bo‘lishi mumkin. Qaror qilishingizdan oldin qo‘shimcha xarajat bo‘lsa, albatta tushuntiraman.

## 3. Quote and decision templates

### A. Sending a quote
**English**
> Based on the details you shared, the estimated price for [scope] is [price and currency]. This includes [included items] and does not include [excluded items, if any]. The estimate is valid until [real date, if applicable]. Would you like me to clarify anything?

**O‘zbekcha**
> Siz yuborgan ma’lumotlarga ko‘ra, [ish hajmi] uchun taxminiy narx [narx va valyuta]. Narxga [kiritilgan ishlar] kiradi, [kiritilmagan ishlar] esa kirmaydi. Taklif [haqiqiy sana]gacha amal qiladi. Biror joyini tushuntirib berishim kerakmi?

### B. Customer is comparing options
**English**
> That makes sense. The main difference between [Option A] and [Option B] is [truthful difference]. If your priority is [customer priority], I’d suggest [option] because [specific reason]. There’s no pressure to decide now.

**O‘zbekcha**
> Tushunarli. [A-variant] va [B-variant] o‘rtasidagi asosiy farq — [haqiqiy farq]. Agar siz uchun [mijoz ustuvorligi] muhim bo‘lsa, [aniq sabab] tufayli [variant]ni tavsiya qilaman. Hozir qaror qilishga majbur emassiz.

### C. Gentle check-in after a quote
**English**
> Hi [Name], just checking whether you had a chance to review the estimate I sent on [date]. I’m happy to answer questions or adjust the scope if your needs have changed. If you’ve decided not to proceed, that’s completely fine too.

**O‘zbekcha**
> Salom [Ism], [sana] kuni yuborgan hisobimizni ko‘rib chiqishga vaqtingiz bo‘ldimi, deb so‘ramoqchi edim. Savollaringiz bo‘lsa javob beraman yoki ehtiyojingiz o‘zgargan bo‘lsa, ish hajmini qayta ko‘rib chiqamiz. Agar davom etmaslikka qaror qilgan bo‘lsangiz ham, bu mutlaqo tabiiy.

## 4. Delivery and after-service templates

### A. Confirming the booking
**English**
> Your [appointment/order] is confirmed for [date] at [time]. The agreed scope is [scope], and the agreed price is [price]. Please let me know as early as possible if you need to change the time.

**O‘zbekcha**
> [Uchrashuv/buyurtma]ingiz [sana] kuni soat [vaqt]ga tasdiqlandi. Kelishilgan ish hajmi: [ish], narxi: [narx]. Vaqtni o‘zgartirish kerak bo‘lsa, imkon qadar oldin xabar bering.

### B. Work completed
**English**
> Hi [Name], the [service/order] is complete. We completed: [short factual list]. Please review [delivery/location/link]. If anything within the agreed scope needs attention, tell me by [reasonable date/process].

**O‘zbekcha**
> Salom [Ism], [xizmat/buyurtma] tayyor. Bajarilgan ishlar: [qisqa va aniq ro‘yxat]. Natijani [topshirish joyi/havola] orqali ko‘rishingiz mumkin. Kelishilgan ish doirasida e’tibor talab qiladigan joy bo‘lsa, [oqilona sana/tartib] bo‘yicha xabar bering.

### C. Feedback request — optional
**English**
> Thanks for choosing [Business]. If you’re comfortable sharing, what worked well and what could we improve? Feedback is optional and helps us improve the service. Please don’t include private payment or identity information.

**O‘zbekcha**
> [Biznes]ni tanlaganingiz uchun rahmat. Agar xohlasangiz, qaysi jihatlar yaxshi bo‘lgani va nimani yaxshilashimiz mumkinligini yozib qoldiring. Fikr bildirish ixtiyoriy. Iltimos, shaxsiy to‘lov yoki hujjat ma’lumotlarini yubormang.

## 5. A respectful 7-day follow-up schedule

Use this only when the person has made an enquiry and has not asked you to stop. Adjust timing to the customer's expectations and your business hours.

| Timing | Action | Message purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Reply to the enquiry | Answer the question and request only essential details |
| Day 1 | If details are missing, one short check-in | Ask whether they need help providing the details |
| Day 2–3 | If a quote was sent, one check-in | Offer clarification; make it easy to decline |
| Day 5 | Optional final helpful note | Share one relevant detail or answer a known concern |
| Day 7 | Close the loop | Say you will close the enquiry unless they want to continue |

**Final close-the-loop message — English**
> Hi [Name], I haven’t heard back, so I’ll close this enquiry for now to avoid filling your inbox. If you’d like to continue later, just message me. Thank you for considering [Business].

**Yakuniy xabar — O‘zbekcha**
> Salom [Ism], javob bo‘lmagani uchun xabarlaringizni ko‘paytirmaslik maqsadida hozircha murojaatni yopaman. Keyinroq davom ettirmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, bemalol yozing. [Biznes]ni ko‘rib chiqqaningiz uchun rahmat.

## 7. Weekly 15-minute review

- Count new enquiries received.
- Count enquiries answered.
- Count quotes sent.
- Count customers who chose to proceed.
- Note the three most common questions.
- Improve one template based on real customer feedback.
- Check that anyone who opted out is marked “Do not contact”.

A simple response-rate calculation:
**Reply rate = enquiries answered ÷ total enquiries × 100**

A simple quote acceptance calculation:
**Quote acceptance rate = accepted quotes ÷ quotes sent × 100**

Use these as internal tracking measures, not as proof that the templates caused a result. Compare similar periods and avoid drawing conclusions from very small samples.
